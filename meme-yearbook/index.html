A Note From the Principal

Dear Class of 2025,

When you first walked—or clipped through the drywall—into Meme Valley High four years ago, nobody knew how a student body made of 16-bit sprites, shaky MS Paint doodles, early-2000s CGI renders, and at least two sentient household objects could ever share one cafeteria.

Yet here you are: weird, unbothered, and somehow still passing AP Physics. Whether you are heading to college, going straight into a group chat reaction folder, or remaining permanently at 96×120 resolution, never forget who you were on Picture Day.

Congratulations, Seniors. Remember to save your work.

Dr. Arthur Raster Principal, Meme Valley High School
