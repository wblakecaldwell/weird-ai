# weird-ai

A collection of fun, whimsical, and mildly bizarre AI experiments—mostly taking ridiculous ideas slightly too seriously.

No grand roadmap, no productivity hacks—just strange little projects poking at what local models, LLMs, and image generators can do when pointed at weird ideas.

## Experiments

| Project | Description | Stack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`meme-yearbook`](./meme-yearbook) | **Meme Valley High School — Class of 2025**: a real-looking high school yearbook populated entirely by original, AI-invented mixed-media meme characters (pixel art, cursed 3D, doodles, and more). | LM Studio, `mflux` (MLX), Python, HTML/CSS |

## Philosophy

- Follow weird ideas to completion.
- Prefer local models and reproducible pipelines where possible.
- Treat absurd premises with a straight face.
